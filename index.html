About Anna Cafe

A journey inspired by the flavours and culture of South India

Our South Indian Story

Anna Cafe is inspired by the colourful streets, temples and traditional kitchens of South India.

We believe that good food brings people together. Our kitchen prepares classic South Indian favourites using traditional recipes and fresh ingredients.

Enjoy a crispy dosa, soft idli, hot sambar and a cup of freshly prepared filter coffee in a warm and friendly atmosphere.

Explore South India

The inspiration behind our cafe

Tamil Nadu

Famous for beautiful Dravidian temples, colourful gopurams and rich food traditions.

Kerala

Known for coconut-rich cuisine, banana-leaf meals, greenery and beautiful traditional culture.

Karnataka & South India

Home to historic temples, traditional coffee culture and delicious vegetarian cuisine.
